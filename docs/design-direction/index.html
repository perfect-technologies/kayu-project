KAYOU — design direction

Last updated 2026-05-12 · Living document

Captures the visual decisions and learnings established during the landing-page redesign (2026-05) and applied to every page going forward. Pair with the design tokens in packages/ui/src/tokens.ts and the structural rules in docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.

The intent is direction, not specification — what we choose and what we explicitly reject, with enough reasoning that future work doesn't have to re-litigate it.

Hard rules

Apply to every surface unless a section below explicitly carves out an exception.

  1. No gradient backgrounds. Not on cards, not on sections, not behind CTAs. The only gradient on the marketing pages is the hero headline text (kept on purpose — it's the brand). Replaced everywhere else by flat surfaces (white, beige #F5F2E9, or warm off-white #FFFBF5 for the provider CTA only).
  2. Lucide icons only. No emoji-as-icon, no other icon sets. A single decorative emoji is allowed on section overlines (🔥, ⭐, ✨, 📱) — that's it.
  3. Plain text for secondary info. Counts, metadata, response times, missions, etc. are text — not chips, not pills. The "X pros" count under a category tile is just text.
  4. Restrained color. Color is anchored to one specific element per section, not sprayed across the surface. The categories grid is intentionally fully monochrome.
  5. Photography over illustration when data supports it. Real category and provider photos make the marketplace feel real. Icons step in only when no image exists.

Mockup discipline

The provider dashboard preview, the phone mockups, and the how-it-works step mockups are all built from divs, not screenshots.

Why this matters:

  • Sharp at any size; doesn't blur on retina or break on dark mode.
  • Re-translates automatically when copy changes (e.g., "Plomberie" → "Plumbing" in i18n).
  • No screenshot maintenance when the real app evolves.
  • Smaller bundle.

This is the rule going forward for any "look at our product" visual in marketing surfaces.

Trending / discovery behavior

A single API endpoint (GET /stats/trending-services) returns one of two modes:

  • Trending when ≥ 3 categories have bookings in the last 7 days AND have a Category.image set. Ranked by booking count, with a growth-% badge (≥ 10% growth → growth badge, otherwise "Top N" badge).
  • Discovery as fallback. Same card layout, header changes to "✨ À DÉCOUVRIR / Catégories à découvrir", trend badge omitted.

Categories without an image are excluded from both modes. If zero categories qualify at all, the section is hidden entirely — never a placeholder card.

startingPrice on the card is the minimum Provider.hourlyRate across active providers in that category (not average, not from quotes). This communicates "à partir de X FC" honestly.

10-minute in-memory cache on the controller — refreshes slowly, no per-request DB hit on busy pages.

What we explicitly rejected

These came up during brainstorming and we chose not to do them. Documenting so they don't get re-proposed:

  • Dark glassmorphism / mesh-gradient backgrounds (the old kayu prototype's style).
  • Per-category colored tile backgrounds on the category grid — picked monochrome. Color is too noisy across 12 tiles.
  • Colored category-icon "tile" on provider cards with a floating circular mini-avatar — replaced with a flat avatar + categories in chips.
  • Chips/pills around stat counts — plain text reads better.
  • Single big rotating testimonial with autoplay — picked 3-quotes-at-once. Autoplay is annoying and people miss content.
  • "+N more" overflow when listing a provider's secondary categories — show all of them.
  • Filled bold buttons inside cards (Réserver, Voir le profil) — picked text-only with arrow.
  • Aspirational placeholder images / gradient fallbacks when a category has no image. We hide the card instead.
